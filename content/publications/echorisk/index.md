This work explores how large echocardiography foundation models can be adapted
to downstream cardio-oncology risk prediction tasks.

The proposed solution combines task-specific fine-tuning, multi-view modeling,
model ensembling, and test-time augmentation.

I led a four-researcher team, served as first and corresponding author, and led
experimental integration, results analysis, and manuscript preparation.
